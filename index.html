Who We Are

About Jivan Joti Nursing College

A legacy of excellence in nursing education since 1998, committed to shaping compassionate healthcare leaders.

Our Rich History

Established in 1998, Jivan Joti Nursing College was founded with a singular vision: to create healthcare professionals who combine technical expertise with deep human compassion. Over the past 25+ years, we have graduated more than 8,000 nurses who serve in top hospitals across India and internationally.

Affiliated with the State University of Health Sciences and approved by the Indian Nursing Council (INC) and the State Nursing Council, our institution maintains the highest academic and clinical standards.

Our Vision

To be the premier institution of nursing education in South Asia, producing skilled, ethical, and compassionate healthcare professionals who transform patient care and advance the nursing profession globally.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional nursing education through evidence-based curricula, state-of-the-art simulation labs, and extensive clinical training — nurturing graduates who lead with knowledge, skill, and heart.

Our Nursing Courses

Choose from our INC-approved programs designed to build clinical competency and professional excellence.

Most Popular

B.Sc Nursing

A comprehensive 4-year undergraduate program that trains students in all aspects of modern nursing practice, from anatomy to advanced patient care management.

Duration: 4 Years + 6 Months Internship
Eligibility: 10+2 with PCB, Min. 45% marks
Intake: 60 Seats per batch
Program Highlights
  • Advanced clinical simulation training
  • Community health nursing internship
  • Research methodology & evidence-based practice
  • Direct entry to M.Sc Nursing programs
Apply for B.Sc Nursing

GNM – General Nursing & Midwifery

A 3.5-year diploma program that provides strong foundations in general nursing and midwifery, preparing graduates for immediate clinical deployment in hospitals and community settings.

Duration: 3.5 Years (incl. 6 month internship)
Eligibility: 10+2 with any stream, Min. 40%
Intake: 80 Seats per batch
Program Highlights
  • Comprehensive midwifery training
  • OT & ICU clinical exposure
  • Maternal and child health nursing
  • Direct hospital placement support
Apply for GNM

ANM – Auxiliary Nursing Midwifery

A 2-year community-focused diploma program designed to produce skilled healthcare workers proficient in maternal and child health, family planning, and primary healthcare delivery.

Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: 10+2 (any stream), Min. 40%
Intake: 60 Seats per batch
Program Highlights
  • Primary healthcare delivery training
  • Rural health outreach programs
  • Family planning & nutrition counseling
  • Government sector placement pathways
Apply for ANM

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about admissions, programs, and campus life.

The last date for online application submission is August 31, 2025. However, seats are filled on a rolling basis, so early application is strongly encouraged. Applicants are advised to apply as soon as the notification is released.

Admission to the B.Sc Nursing program is based on the State Common Entrance Test (CET) score or merit of 12th grade marks as per state government norms. Our institution also considers the NEET UG score for some seats. Detailed merit list criteria are published each year.

Yes. Jivan Joti Nursing College offers several scholarship schemes: Merit Scholarship (for top 10% scorers), EWS Scholarship (income-based), State Government SC/ST/OBC scholarships, and Management Scholarship. Students can also avail bank education loans through our tied-up banks.

We have separate hostels for boys and girls, each with 24/7 security. Rooms are furnished (2-3 per room) with a study desk, wardrobe, and internet connectivity. The mess provides vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals. There's a separate warden for each hostel, a common TV room, and a reading lounge.

Absolutely. Our B.Sc Nursing degree is recognized by nursing councils in the USA (NCLEX-RN), UK (NMC), Australia (AHPRA), and the Gulf countries. We also offer dedicated coaching for NCLEX-RN and NMC-CBT exams. Several of our alumni are currently working in the USA, UK, Canada, UAE, and Australia.

We maintain an INC-mandated faculty-to-student ratio of 1:10 for theory classes and 1:5 for clinical training, ensuring personalized attention for every student. We currently have 120+ qualified faculty members for approximately 2,500 students across all programs.
